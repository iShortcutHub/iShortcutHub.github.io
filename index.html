Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about safety, compatibility, or installation?

Are these shortcuts completely safe to install?

Yes! All listed shortcuts use official Apple iCloud share links. We manually review scripts to make sure they do not compromise your privacy or data.

Why does it say "Untrusted Shortcut" when I click install?

Apple requires a toggle enabled in your iPhone settings for community-shared scripts. Simply run at least one default shortcut on your device or enable untrusted scripts in Settings.

Can I submit my own shortcuts to this library?

Absolutely! Click the "Publish" button on the navigation bar to submit your shortcut details right onto the platform.
